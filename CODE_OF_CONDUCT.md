# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment, and keep discussion about the work.

It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space. Report unacceptable behaviour privately
to the maintainer at CONDUCT_CONTACT. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or
block participants who break it.
